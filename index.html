FAQ

Questions people ask us

How much does a solar system cost?

It depends on what you want to power and for how long. A small inverter backup for a flat costs far less than a commercial system for a factory. After your free assessment and site survey, we give you a fixed quotation.

Will it work at night and on cloudy days?

Yes. Batteries store energy for the night, and hybrid systems can also charge from the grid. We size the battery to cover the hours you need.

Do I still need my generator?

Most customers use it far less, or keep it only as an emergency backup. Off-grid and commercial designs are planned so the generator becomes the last resort.

How long does installation take?

Most home systems are installed in one to two days after the survey. Larger commercial systems are scheduled in phases to avoid disrupting your business.

What happens after I submit the form?

You get a reference number straight away. Our team reviews your request, and you hear from us by email or phone. Urgent requests are contacted first.
